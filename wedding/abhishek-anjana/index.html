A Love Story in Six Chapters

Our Story

Some stories begin with a grand gesture. Theirs began with a quiet glance inside a temple in Kerala.

❧ॐ❧
Chapter 01

The Temple Bell

Where destiny knocked

It was an ordinary day at the temple. The fragrance of jasmine drifted through the courtyard, lamps flickered near the sanctum, and somewhere between the prayers and the temple bells, their eyes met for the very first time.

There were no long conversations that day. Just a passing glance, a shy smile and a moment that neither could quite forget. They walked away as strangers, unaware that a beautiful story had quietly begun.

“ആദ്യ കാഴ്ച - അമ്പലത്തിൽ വെച്ച്, ഈശ്വരൻ എഴുതിയ തുടക്കം.”
Chapter 02

The Introduction

A hello that changed everything

A little introduction brought them together again. The familiar face from the temple now had a name — Abhishek. And the smile he remembered belonged to Anjana. A simple hello opened the door to a thousand conversations.

At first, the conversations were small and careful. But every hello made the next one easier. Like the first rain after summer, something gentle had started to grow.

“Some meetings are just a beginning. This one was a promise.”
Chapter 03

Phone Calls & Midnight Messages

Where friendship turned to love

A “Good morning” became the start of each day; a “Good night” became the last message before sleep. Phone calls stretched late into the night, messages carried little smiles through busy days, and even the simplest moments felt special.

Did you reach home safely? 11:52 pm

Somewhere between those late calls and early messages, friendship quietly took off its disguise and became love.

Chapter 04

Two Months, One Answer

When the heart said “yes”

After two months of calls, messages and getting to know each other, their feelings had found a name. It was time to take the next step — together.

With hopeful hearts, they spoke to their families about the person who had become so important. It was a moment filled with anticipation, prayers and the hope that everyone would see what they saw in each other.

“Ee snehathinu veettukaarude anugraham venam.”
Chapter 05

And Everyone Said Yes

Blessings, tears and sweet laughter

And the answer was happiness. Their families welcomed the relationship with love and blessings. What began as two people getting to know each other slowly became a bond between two families.

There were smiles, warm conversations and the comfort of knowing that the people they loved were happy too. With everyone’s blessings, their journey towards marriage began.

“Ellaarum sammathichu. Ini ividam muthal oru yathra - onnichu.”
Chapter 06

The Wedding Chapter

The best chapter is just beginning

From one glance at a temple to a promise before family: every call, every message and every blessing has led them here. The nadaswaram is ready, the nilavilakku is glowing, and a new chapter is waiting.

On Thursday, 26 November 2026, Abhishek and Anjana begin their married life. They warmly invite you and your family to join them and bless their new beginning.
