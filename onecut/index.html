01 / THE IDEA

Everything starts
with a cut.

OneCut is a desktop-first video editing project from Oru Design, shaped around a simple belief: creative tools should keep you close to the work.

Bring your media into one workspace, arrange your sequence, work with sound and visuals, and move toward a finished export — without losing the rhythm of your idea.

DESIGNED FOR THE NEXT FRAME.
01
▤

Timeline-first editing

Arrange clips and layers in a visual editing space designed to keep your sequence in focus.
